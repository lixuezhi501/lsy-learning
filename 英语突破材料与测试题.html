第一部分：解题技巧

一、完形填空技巧

1. 通读全文，把握大意（最重要！）

  • 拿到题目后，不要急着填空，先快速通读全文（跳过硬要填的空），了解文章大意和基本情感走向。
  • 第一遍读完后，你应该能回答：这篇文章讲了什么？是记叙文、说明文还是议论文？

2. 根据上下文推断（Context Clues）

  • 前文提示：空格前面句子提供的信息往往能帮助判断。
  • 后文暗示：有时答案在后文才揭晓，要耐心往下读。
  • 复现词：文章中重复出现的关键词或同义词往往是答案线索。

二、阅读理解技巧

1. 快速浏览题干（Question First）

  • 先读问题，带着问题去读文章，效率更高。
  • 注意题干中的关键词（人名、地名、数字、专有名词等）。

2. 定位关键信息（Scanning）

  • 找到题干中的关键词后，在文章中快速定位对应段落。
  • 答案通常在关键词所在句的附近，一般不超过前一句和后一句。
  • 注意同义替换：选项往往不直接用文章原词，而是用同义词替换。

3. 推理判断方法（Inferring）

  • 推理题不能直接找到答案，需要根据文章信息推断。
  • 切忌过度推理：只能根据文章提供的信息推理，不能加入自己的主观想法。
  • 关键词：infer, imply, suggest, probably, most likely。

4. 主旨大意归纳（Main Idea）

  • 主旨题通常看文章首段和尾段，以及每段的第一句。
  • 正确的主旨选项应该能概括全文，而非只针对某一段。
  • 关键词：main idea, mainly about, the best title, the purpose。

三、作文技巧

1. 审题步骤

  1. 确定文体：记叙文（写人/记事）、说明文、应用文（书信/通知/日记）
  2. 确定时态：一般现在时、一般过去时、一般将来时
  3. 确定人称：第一人称（I/We）、第三人称（He/She/They）
  4. 列出要点：根据题目要求，用中文列出必须包含的内容要点

2. 段落结构（汉堡包结构）

段落功能建议长度
开头段引出主题，吸引读者1-2句
主体段展开论述/叙述，2-3个要点4-6句
结尾段总结全文，点题升华1-2句

3. 过渡词使用（务必使用！）

  • 表示顺序：First, Second, Third, Finally
  • 表示递进：What's more, Besides, In addition, Also
  • 表示转折：However, But, On the other hand
  • 表示总结：In a word, All in all, In short, In conclusion
  • 表示举例：For example, Such as, Like

第二部分：作文万能句型

句型1：开篇引入句型

"With the development of society, ... has become more and more important."

翻译：随着社会的发展，……变得越来越重要。

使用场景：议论文/说明文的开篇

示例：With the development of society, learning English has become more and more important.

句型2：表达观点句型

"In my opinion, ... / As far as I am concerned, ..."

翻译：在我看来 / 就我而言

使用场景：表达自己的观点

示例：In my opinion, reading is a good way to relax.

句型3：列举原因/理由

"There are several reasons why..."

翻译：有以下几个原因说明为什么……

使用场景：说明文或议论文中列举原因

示例：There are several reasons why I like my school.

句型4："不仅……而且……"

"Not only... but also..."

翻译：不仅……而且……

使用场景：表达两个并列的优点或特点

示例：She not only sings well but also dances beautifully.

句型5：表达好处/优点

"... is helpful / useful / good for..."

翻译：……对……有帮助/有用/有好处

使用场景：描述某事物的好处

示例：Doing sports is good for our health.

句型6：表达对比

"Compared with..., ..." / "On the one hand..., on the other hand..."

翻译：与……相比 / 一方面……另一方面……

使用场景：进行对比分析

示例：Compared with watching TV, reading books is more meaningful.

句型7：表达建议

"We should... / It's a good idea to... / Why not...?"

翻译：我们应该…… / 做……是个好主意 / 为什么不……？

使用场景：提建议

示例：Why not join an English club to practice speaking?

句型8：描述感受

"I feel... when... / It makes me feel..."

翻译：当……时，我感觉…… / 这让我感觉……

使用场景：记叙文中表达情感

示例：I feel happy when I help others.

句型9：总结句型

"In a word, ... / All in all, ... / In short, ..."

翻译：总之 / 总而言之 / 简而言之

使用场景：文章结尾总结

示例：In a word, a healthy lifestyle is very important for everyone.

句型10：表达期望/愿望

"I hope that... / I'm looking forward to... / I wish..."

翻译：我希望…… / 我期待…… / 我祝愿……

使用场景：文章结尾表达期望

示例：I hope that my dream will come true one day.

第三部分：作文范文

范文一：记叙文 —— "My Summer Holiday"

My Summer Holiday


Summer holiday is coming. I am so excited because I have made a great plan for it.


First, I am going to finish my homework in the first two weeks. I think it is important to study hard even during the holiday. Second, I plan to read at least three books. My favorite kind of books is science fiction. Reading can open my mind and help me learn more about the world. Third, I will spend some time doing sports with my friends. We are going to play basketball and swim together. Not only can sports keep us healthy, but they can also make us happy.


Besides, my parents and I will visit my grandparents in the countryside. I always have a wonderful time there. The air is fresh and the people are friendly.


In a word, I believe this summer holiday will be meaningful and colorful. I am really looking forward to it!


亮点分析

  • 时态：使用一般将来时（be going to / will），表达计划。
  • 结构：开头引入 → 分点叙述计划 → 额外亮点 → 总结期待。
  • 过渡词：First, Second, Third, Besides, In a word。
  • 句型：Not only...but also...（第3点）、我期待（结尾）。
  • 字数：约 120 词，适合七年级作文要求。

范文二：说明文 —— "How to Learn English Well"

How to Learn English Well


English is one of the most important subjects in school. Many students find it difficult, but I believe everyone can learn it well if they use the right ways. Here are my suggestions.


First, practice listening every day. You can listen to English songs or watch English cartoons. It is a fun way to improve your listening skills. Second, read more English books and stories. When you meet new words, try to guess their meanings from the context. You can also write them down in a notebook. Third, don't be afraid of speaking. Join an English club or talk with your classmates in English. Remember, practice makes perfect!


What's more, it is important to review what you have learned regularly. You can make a study plan and follow it every day.


All in all, learning English needs time and hard work. But if you never give up, you will surely make great progress!


亮点分析

  • 时态：使用一般现在时，说明事实和建议。
  • 结构：引入问题 → 具体建议（3条）→ 补充建议 → 鼓励总结。
  • 过渡词：First, Second, Third, What's more, All in all。
  • 谚语：Practice makes perfect（熟能生巧）。
  • 字数：约 130 词。

I usually get up at 6:30 in the morning. After breakfast, I go to school by bike. It takes me about 20 minutes. We have four classes in the morning and three in the afternoon.

  • 字数：约80词（不含开头结尾），符合要求。
